HOW TO ADD YOUR FACEBOOK / TIKTOK LINKS (no coding)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the admin panel (/admin/) and connect as usual.
2. Go to the "Brand & Contact" section.
3. Paste your full Facebook page link into "Facebook Page URL"
   (e.g. https://facebook.com/YourPage).
4. Paste your full TikTok profile link into "TikTok Profile URL"
   (e.g. https://tiktok.com/@yourhandle).
5. Save Changes. The icons appear in the website footer automatically.
   Clear a field and save to hide that icon again.

generated by the commands above.)
