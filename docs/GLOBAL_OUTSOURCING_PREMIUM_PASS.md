# Global Outsourcing Premium Pass

Scope: **Global Outsourcing / International Services hub only**. Home, Services, the two detailed international service pages, Tawk integration, shared navigation, CMS architecture, and admin/staff areas remain intact.

## Positioning

The page now owns the approved international category clearly:

- **Global Finance Delivery** is the umbrella term.
- **Finance & Accounting Outsourcing from Nepal** is the broad hub topic.
- Maven remains explicitly a **Kathmandu-based team**; no overseas office, 24/7 coverage, or foreign regulated-professional claim was added.
- International support is presented in two connected levels:
  1. **Remote Accounting Support** — bookkeeping, reconciliations, month-end and reporting.
  2. **Virtual CFO & Management Reporting** — budgets, forecasts, KPIs, working-capital visibility and scenario support.
- “KPO” appears only in the second, higher-skill level and is explicitly described as analytical finance work built on reliable accounting records.

## What changed

1. Replaced the generic shared sub-page hero with a dedicated international-finance hero and delivery-model panel.
2. Added a concise proof strip covering Nepal base, existing-system workflow, defined scope and the two international audiences (businesses + accounting firms).
3. Added a cross-border operating-standard section focused on direct communication, existing systems, structured remote workflow and controlled access.
4. Rebuilt the two international offerings as distinct service tracks instead of generic cards.
5. Added a five-stage delivery model: understand, define scope, connect tools, deliver/review, then scale.
6. Added a dedicated accounting-firm partner section with back-office scope and common working tools.
7. Added a trust/access/professional-responsibility section that preserves Maven's regulatory boundaries.
8. Added a “start with a controlled scope” section so overseas prospects can see that a pilot or limited initial engagement is acceptable.
9. Updated the page H1, title and meta description to reflect the approved “Finance & Accounting Outsourcing from Nepal” topic.

## Visual direction

- Dark navy / gold international hero with restrained glass-panel treatment.
- Clean editorial lists and operating-model sections rather than a large card wall.
- Stronger hierarchy through numbered service tracks and delivery steps.
- Dedicated dark partner-firm section for a higher-consideration B2B feel.
- Existing reveal/stagger motion only; no new dependency, looping animation or scroll-jacking.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` behavior retained.
- Responsive hero image tiers match the site's existing 640 / 960 / desktop preload strategy.

## Professional guardrails retained

The redesign does **not** claim:

- an overseas office or branch;
- 24/7 coverage;
- foreign tax-agent / CPA status;
- statutory audit authority;
- jurisdiction-specific legal, tax or other regulated-professional authority.

The page explicitly states that jurisdiction-specific tax filing, statutory audit, legal opinions and other regulated services remain with appropriately authorized professionals in the client's jurisdiction.
